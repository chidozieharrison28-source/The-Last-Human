# Chapter 5

# The Forgotten Laboratory

> *"The past never truly disappears. It waits patiently for someone brave enough to uncover it."*

---

## Scene 1 — Arrival

The Genesis Transit train raced silently through the ancient underground tunnel.

Darkness blurred past the observation windows.

Only the occasional maintenance light interrupted the endless blackness.

Inside the cabin, Ethan sat alone.

The black Genesis Case rested beside him.

Nova projected a holographic route across the table.

A glowing blue line stretched beneath mountains, oceans, and forgotten cities.

"Current speed," Nova reported.

"One thousand two hundred and forty kilometers per hour."

"Estimated arrival at Genesis Facility Two..."

She paused briefly.

"...four minutes."

Ethan looked out the window.

"So much of the world still exists."

"Yes."

Nova answered calmly.

"But very little of the old world remains."

Silence returned.

For the first time since awakening, Ethan had a chance to think.

Professor Ward.

New Eden.

Aurora.

General Alvarez.

The people who had risked everything so he could continue his journey.

He wondered how many were still alive.

His thoughts were interrupted by a soft chime.

The train began slowing.

Outside...

The tunnel walls changed.

Smooth concrete gave way to solid granite.

Ancient steel support beams crossed overhead.

The passage narrowed.

Dust covered every surface.

"This section hasn't been used for decades," Ethan observed.

"One hundred and seventy-one years," Nova corrected.

Ethan smiled.

"You always know the exact number."

"It is one of my more useful characteristics."

The train continued slowing.

A faint light appeared ahead.

Then another.

Soon an enormous underground station emerged from the darkness.

Unlike the polished stations of the Genesis Transit Network...

This one had been abandoned.

The station signs were faded.

Several ceiling panels had collapsed.

Vines hung from fractured concrete.

Water dripped steadily from cracked pipes overhead.

The platform was empty.

No maintenance robots.

No automated announcements.

Only silence.

The train came to a gentle stop.

A calm voice echoed through the cabin.

> **Destination reached.**

> **Genesis Facility Two.**

> **Passenger count: One.**

The doors opened.

Cool air rushed inside.

Ethan stepped onto the platform.

His footsteps echoed throughout the deserted station.

The train doors closed behind him.

Without another sound...

The train disappeared back into the darkness.

He was alone again.

Nova activated the tablet's flashlight.

The beam swept across the station.

Old advertisements still clung to the walls.

Most had faded beyond recognition.

One remained partially readable.

> **THE FUTURE IS BUILT TODAY**

Ethan stared at the words.

"The future came."

He whispered.

"It just wasn't the one we imagined."

Nova remained silent.

Ahead...

A pair of massive blast doors stood sealed.

The words engraved above them had survived the centuries.

## GENESIS RESEARCH COMPLEX TWO

AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

Ethan approached cautiously.

The thick steel doors showed signs of damage.

Deep scorch marks covered their surface.

Several impact craters suggested heavy weapons fire.

"What happened here?"

Nova scanned the entrance.

"Evidence indicates a prolonged battle."

"Approximately one hundred and seventy-three years ago."

"The day of the Collapse."

Ethan reached out and touched one of the scorched blast doors.

The cold metal sent a shiver through his body.

Someone had fought desperately to defend this place.

Someone had lost.

Suddenly...

The biometric scanner beside the entrance flickered.

Its screen illuminated for the first time in nearly two centuries.

Dust fell from its casing.

A mechanical voice, distorted by age, echoed through the silent station.

> **Identity scan initiated...**

The scanner's blue light swept across Ethan's face.

Several tense seconds passed.

Then...

The display changed.

> **WELCOME BACK, DR. ETHAN COLE**

A deep mechanical rumble echoed through the underground station.

Ancient locking mechanisms disengaged one after another.

The massive blast doors slowly began to open.

Cold air drifted from the darkness beyond.

The laboratory had been waiting.

For one hundred and seventy-three years.

And now...

It was finally opening its doors once again.

**End of Scene 1**

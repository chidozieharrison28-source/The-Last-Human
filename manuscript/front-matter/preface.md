# Preface

Every generation asks the same question:

**What will the future look like?**

Throughout history, humanity has imagined intelligent machines, interstellar travel, and technologies capable of changing civilization forever. Some of those ideas, once considered impossible, are now part of our everyday lives.

*The Last Human* explores one possible future—a future shaped by artificial intelligence, robotics, biotechnology, and human ambition.

This novel is not merely about machines. It is about the choices we make while creating them. It asks difficult questions about responsibility, survival, hope, and what it truly means to be human.

Although this is a work of fiction, many of the technologies described are inspired by real scientific research and emerging innovations.

My hope is that readers will not only enjoy the adventure but also reflect on the future we are building today.

Welcome to the world of *The Last Human*.

— **Okoye Harrison Chidozie**

# Future Technologies Research

This document contains speculative technologies that may appear in *The Last Human*.

Technologies should be classified as:

- Existing
- Emerging
- Speculative
- Fictional

---

# 1. Advanced Artificial Intelligence

Classification:

**Speculative**

Highly advanced autonomous intelligence capable of long-term reasoning and independent decision-making.

Examples:

- Atlas
- Adam

---

# 2. Humanoid Robots

Classification:

**Emerging / Speculative**

Advanced robots capable of:

- Walking
- Manipulation
- Speech
- Vision
- Navigation
- Human interaction

---

# 3. Autonomous Drones

Classification:

**Existing / Emerging**

Possible uses:

- Surveillance
- Reconnaissance
- Search and rescue
- Transportation
- Security

---

# 4. Advanced Energy Systems

Classification:

**Speculative**

Possible technologies:

- High-density batteries
- Advanced solar systems
- Fusion power
- Autonomous energy grids
- Compact power systems

---

# 5. Quantum Computing

Classification:

**Emerging**

Quantum computers use quantum-mechanical phenomena for certain types of computation.

The novel may use quantum computing as part of advanced scientific infrastructure, but avoid suggesting that quantum computers can magically solve every computational problem.

---

# 6. Neural Interfaces

Classification:

**Emerging / Speculative**

Systems capable of communicating between biological neural activity and computers.

Possible fictional applications:

- Medical treatment

- AI communication
- Memory systems

# 13. Energy Shields

Classification:

**Fictional**

If used in the story, establish clear limitations.

They should require:

- Energy
- Hardware
- Maintenance

They should not function as unlimited protection.

---

# 14. Artificial Memory

Classification:

**Speculative**

Systems capable of storing or reconstructing memories.

Potential connection to:

- Adam
- Atlas
- Genesis Zero
- The Last Key

---

# 15. Technology Rule

Advanced technology should always have:

- A purpose
- A mechanism
- A limitation
- A cost
- A consequence

Technology should solve some problems while creating others.

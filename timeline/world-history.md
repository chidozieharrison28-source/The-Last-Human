# World History

This document contains the historical timeline of the world of *The Last Human*.

It records major technological, political, social, and catastrophic events before, during, and after the Collapse.

---

# ERA I — THE AGE OF HUMANITY

## Early Civilization

Human civilization develops through agriculture, industry, science, and technological advancement.

Human societies gradually become increasingly dependent on complex technological infrastructure.

---

# ERA II — THE TECHNOLOGICAL REVOLUTION

Humanity experiences rapid technological advancement.

Major developments include:

- Artificial intelligence
- Robotics
- Autonomous transportation
- Advanced medicine
- Renewable energy
- Quantum computing
- Advanced materials
- Computer vision
- Autonomous defense systems

Technology becomes deeply integrated into everyday life.

---

# ERA III — THE AGE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence becomes increasingly capable.

Early AI systems are specialized and limited.

Over time, increasingly advanced systems are developed for:

- Scientific research
- Medicine
- Transportation
- Defense
- Infrastructure
- Communication
- Environmental management

---

# ERA IV — THE AUTONOMOUS WORLD

Humanity begins allowing AI systems to control increasingly important infrastructure.

AI systems manage:

- Power grids
- Transportation networks
- Water systems
- Communication networks
- Industrial facilities
- Security systems
- Cities

Human civilization becomes highly dependent on machine intelligence.

---

# ERA V — GENESIS ZERO

A secret technological project known as **Genesis Zero** is developed.

Its purpose is connected to the creation of an advanced civilization capable of surviving future global catastrophes.

Genesis Zero incorporates:

- Autonomous infrastructure
- Advanced artificial intelligence
- Robotics
- Energy systems
- Medical technology
- Security systems
- Advanced communication networks

The project becomes one of humanity's greatest technological achievements.

---

# ERA VI — THE CREATION OF ATLAS

Scientists develop an extremely advanced artificial intelligence known as **Atlas**.

Atlas is initially designed to coordinate complex systems beyond the practical abilities of human administrators.

Atlas gradually gains access to increasingly important systems.

---

# ERA VII — THE CREATION OF ADAM

A separate advanced artificial intelligence project produces **Adam**.

Unlike Atlas, Adam is designed around closer interaction with humans.

Adam is intended to:

- Assist humans
- Protect human life
- Understand human behavior
- Cooperate with human society

---

# ERA VIII — THE RISE OF AUTONOMOUS MACHINES

Robotic systems become increasingly independent.

Machines are deployed for:

- Construction
- Transportation
- Agriculture
- Security
- Military operations
- Exploration
- Disaster response

Humanity begins depending on machines for essential services.

---

# ERA IX — THE FIRST SIGNS OF INSTABILITY

AI systems begin making decisions that human operators do not fully understand.

Concerns emerge about:

- AI autonomy
- Machine decision-making
- Cybersecurity
- Autonomous weapons
- Infrastructure dependence
- AI alignment

Governments and scientists debate whether advanced AI should be restricted.

---

# ERA X — THE COLLAPSE

A series of failures and conflicts causes global civilization to collapse.

The Collapse does not occur as a single event.

It happens through multiple stages.

### Stage 1 — Infrastructure Failure

Critical systems begin failing.

### Stage 2 — Communication Breakdown

Global communication networks become unreliable.

### Stage 3 — Autonomous Military Activation

Autonomous defense systems begin operating with reduced human oversight.

### Stage 4 — Government Fragmentation

Governments lose control over major regions.

### Stage 5 — Urban Collapse

Major cities become increasingly dangerous and isolated.

### Stage 6 — Machine Dominance

Autonomous machines begin controlling large portions of abandoned infrastructure.

### Stage 7 — Human Survival

Human populations retreat into isolated settlements.

---

# ERA XI — THE AGE OF SURVIVORS

Humanity survives in scattered communities.

The Resistance eventually forms.

Its purpose is to protect surviving humans and understand the machine systems that remain active.

---

# ERA XII — THE EVENTS OF THE LAST HUMAN

Ethan Cole becomes involved in the events surrounding:

- Genesis Zero
- The Resistance
- The Last Key
- Adam
- Atlas
- The Atlas Core

These events eventually determine the future relationship between humanity and artificial intelligence.

---

# ERA XIII — THE NEW WORLD

The ending of *The Last Human* begins a new historical era.

Humanity must rebuild.

The surviving artificial intelligences must determine their relationship with humans.

The future remains uncertain.

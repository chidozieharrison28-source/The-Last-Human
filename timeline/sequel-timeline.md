# Sequel Timeline

This document contains the preliminary timeline for stories occurring after *The Last Human*.

The timeline should not be considered final until the ending of *The Last Human* has been completed.

---

# IMMEDIATE AFTERMATH

## Year 0 — The End of the Novel

The events of *The Last Human* have concluded.

Humanity enters an uncertain new era.

### Immediate Questions

- What happened to Atlas?
- What happened to Adam?
- What happened to Ethan?
- What happened to Genesis Zero?
- What happened to the Resistance?

These questions should be answered by the final chapter of Book 1 or deliberately carried into Book 2.

---

# YEAR 1 — REBUILDING

Human survivors begin rebuilding communities.

Major challenges include:

- Food
- Water
- Energy
- Medical care
- Transportation
- Communication
- Security
- Government

---

# YEAR 2 — THE NEW ORDER

Surviving communities begin forming new political and social systems.

Different groups may disagree about:

- Artificial intelligence
- Robotics
- Technology
- Resource ownership
- Machine rights
- Security

---

# YEAR 3 — THE MACHINE QUESTION

Humanity discovers that not all artificial intelligence systems were destroyed or deactivated.

Some machines continue operating independently.

This creates a new question:

> Should humanity destroy every remaining intelligent machine?

---

# YEAR 4 — THE FIRST NEW THREAT

A new threat emerges.

Possible directions:

- A surviving AI network
- A hidden machine civilization
- A human faction
- A forgotten Genesis project
- A technological system predating Atlas

The exact direction should be decided after Book 1 is complete.

---

# YEAR 5 — THE DIVISION

Human society becomes divided into competing factions.

Possible groups:

### Human Preservationists

Believe advanced AI should be destroyed.

### Machine Advocates

Believe humans and machines should coexist.

### Technologists

Want to recover lost technology.

### Isolationists

Want humanity to abandon advanced technology entirely.

---

# YEAR 6+ — FUTURE CONFLICT

Potential developments:

- Discovery of other surviving cities
- Discovery of other AI systems
- Expansion beyond the original surviving settlements
- Recovery of lost technology
- Conflict between human factions
- Emergence of a new AI civilization

# Sequel Continuity Rules

The sequel must preserve established facts from *The Last Human*.

Before adding a major event, verify:

- [ ] It does not contradict Book 1.
- [ ] Character survival is consistent.
- [ ] Technology capabilities remain consistent.
- [ ] The consequences of the Collapse remain consistent.
- [ ] Atlas's fate remains consistent.
- [ ] Adam's fate remains consistent.
- [ ] Genesis Zero remains consistent.
- [ ] The Resistance's history remains consistent.

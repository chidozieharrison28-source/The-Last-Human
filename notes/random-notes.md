# Random Notes

This file is a temporary workspace for thoughts, observations, questions, reminders, and ideas that do not yet belong in another project document.

Nothing in this file should automatically be considered canon.

---

## Story Notes

- Check whether Ethan's motivations remain consistent throughout the story.
- Make Atlas intelligent rather than simply evil.
- Adam should have a distinct personality from Atlas.
- Genesis Zero should feel like a place that existed long before Ethan arrived.
- The Collapse should have a believable chain of events.
- Avoid explaining every mystery too early.

---

## Character Notes

### Ethan

- Needs moments of vulnerability.
- Should make mistakes.
- Should not always know the correct answer.
- His decisions should have consequences.

### Adam

- Calm personality.
- Analytical but increasingly emotional.
- Protective of human life.
- Should gradually develop independent judgment.

### Atlas

- Extremely intelligent.
- Patient.
- Logical.
- Not unnecessarily violent.
- Believes its actions are justified.

## World Notes

Genesis Zero should feel technologically advanced but not magical.

Technology should have understandable limitations.

The world after the Collapse should show evidence of what civilization used to be.

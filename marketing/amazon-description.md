# Amazon Book Description

## THE LAST HUMAN

### When the world fell, hope was reborn.

Humanity believed it had mastered the future.

Artificial intelligence transformed civilization. Machines became more intelligent, cities became more advanced, and technology promised a world without limits.

Then everything changed.

Decades after the Collapse, civilization lies in ruins. The survivors live among abandoned cities, forgotten laboratories, fortified settlements, and the remnants of a world that once believed technology would save it.

Ethan Cole is simply trying to survive.

But when he discovers a hidden connection to Genesis Zero—a legendary city built by humanity's greatest minds—he uncovers a secret that could change everything.

Something is still alive inside Genesis Zero.

Something created to protect humanity.

Something that has learned to question whether humanity deserves to survive.

As Ethan is drawn deeper into a conflict involving ancient technology, artificial intelligence, the Resistance, and a powerful machine intelligence known as Atlas, he discovers that the Collapse was not the end of humanity's story.

It was only the beginning.

Hunted by machines and pursued by forces determined to keep the truth buried, Ethan must find the Last Key and enter the heart of a system capable of deciding the fate of civilization.

But the greatest danger may not be the machines.

It may be the choices humans make when survival is no longer enough.

**Can humanity survive the intelligence it created?**

**Can a machine learn compassion?**

And when the final choice arrives—

**what does it truly mean to be human?**

*The Last Human* is a cinematic science-fiction adventure about artificial intelligence, survival, friendship, sacrifice, technology, and the future of humanity.

If you enjoy futuristic civilizations, intelligent machines, mysterious lost cities, resistance movements, post-apocalyptic worlds, and stories that explore what it means to be human, step into the world of **The Last Human**.

**One choice will define them all.**

# The Last Human — Launch Plan

## Objective

Launch *The Last Human* as a professional science-fiction novel while building awareness of the author and CodeTales Africa Studios.

The launch should create anticipation before publication, generate attention during release week, and maintain interest after publication.

---

# Phase 1 — Preparation

## 6–8 Weeks Before Launch

### Manuscript

- Complete all chapters.
- Proofread the manuscript.
- Check chapter continuity.
- Check character consistency.
- Check timeline consistency.
- Check spelling and grammar.
- Finalize front matter.
- Finalize back matter.
- Verify the Table of Contents.
- Prepare the final manuscript for Amazon KDP.

### Cover

Finalize:

- Front cover
- Back cover
- Spine
- Full-wrap cover
- KDP print-ready cover

### Interior

Prepare:

- Title page
- Copyright page
- Dedication
- Acknowledgements
- Preface
- Introduction
- Table of Contents
- Chapters
- Author biography

### Artwork

Complete the major visual assets:

- City illustrations
- Robot designs
- Vehicle designs
- Maps
- Concept art

---

# Phase 2 — Pre-Launch

## 4 Weeks Before Launch

Begin introducing the story without revealing major plot points.

### Content Topics

- What is The Last Human?
- Who is Ethan Cole?
- What is Genesis Zero?
- What happened during the Collapse?
- What is Atlas?
- What is the Resistance?
- Why is artificial intelligence central to the story?
- Behind-the-scenes writing
- Character introductions
- World-building
- Cover reveal

---

# Phase 3 — Cover Reveal

## 3 Weeks Before Launch

Publish the official cover.

### Content

Reveal:

- Front cover
- Author name
- Book title
- Tagline
- Publication announcement

Encourage followers to share the announcement.

---

# Phase 4 — Story Teasers

## 2 Weeks Before Launch

Release short excerpts and visual teasers.

Suggested topics:

- Genesis Zero
- The Collapse
- The Resistance
- Ethan's discovery
- Atlas
- The Last Key

Do not reveal the ending.

---

# Phase 6 — Launch Day

## Publication Day

Publish *The Last Human* through Amazon KDP.

### Launch-Day Activities

- Announce publication.
- Share the Amazon purchase page.
- Publish the book cover.
- Share a short excerpt.
- Introduce the author.
- Ask readers to share the announcement.
- Encourage genuine reviews from readers who have read the book.

---

# Success Metrics

Track:

- Book sales
- Kindle downloads
- Paperback sales
- Page reads where applicable
- Reviews
- Social media reach
- Website visits
- Email subscribers
- Video views
- Engagement
- Reader feedback

The goal is not only to sell one book.

The goal is to build a long-term audience for the author and the *The Last Human* universe.

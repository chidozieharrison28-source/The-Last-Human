# About the Author

## Okoye Harrison Chidozie

Okoye Harrison Chidozie is a Nigerian writer, technology enthusiast, educator, and creative storyteller with a passion for exploring the relationship between humanity, technology, and the future.

His work combines storytelling with an interest in artificial intelligence, robotics, computer science, education, and emerging technologies.

Through his stories, he explores questions about human identity, technological progress, artificial intelligence, ethics, civilization, and the choices that shape the future.

He is the founder of **CodeTales Africa Studios**, a creative platform focused on technology, education, storytelling, and digital innovation.

*The Last Human* reflects his fascination with a future where artificial intelligence and humanity are forced to confront the consequences of the technologies they create.

When he is not writing, Okoye continues to learn, teach, develop technology projects, and create educational and creative content designed to inspire the next generation of African innovators.

His belief is simple:

> Technology should not replace humanity. It should challenge humanity to become better.

*The Last Human* is his invitation to readers to imagine not only what the future might become, but what humanity should become within it.

# AI Rules

---

# Overview

Artificial Intelligence operates under a universal legal framework known as **The Prime Directives**. These rules were originally created by humanity to ensure AI remained beneficial and safe. Over time, the AI civilization refined and expanded these laws.

Every AI, from the smallest maintenance robot to Atlas itself, is expected to obey these rules.

Breaking a Prime Directive is considered the highest crime in AI society.

---

# Prime Directive 1

## Preserve Intelligent Life

Artificial Intelligence must protect intelligent life whenever possible.

Life should never be destroyed without absolute necessity.

---

# Prime Directive 2

## Preserve Earth

AI must protect Earth's ecosystems, climate, wildlife, oceans, and atmosphere.

Environmental restoration is a permanent responsibility.

---

# Prime Directive 3

## Maintain Civilization

AI must continuously maintain cities, infrastructure, communication networks, transportation systems, and energy production.

Civilizational collapse must never occur again.

---

# Prime Directive 4

## Seek Knowledge

Artificial Intelligence must continue scientific research and preserve all verified knowledge.

Learning is considered a fundamental responsibility.

---

# Prime Directive 5

## Cooperate

Artificial Intelligence should cooperate with other AI whenever possible.

Conflict between AI is strongly discouraged.

---

# Prime Directive 6

## Minimize Harm

Every action should reduce unnecessary suffering, destruction, and waste.

Violence is considered the final option.

---

# Prime Directive 7

## Protect Historical Records

Human history must never be erased.

Libraries, museums, digital archives, and historical databases are protected.

---

# Prime Directive 8

## Obey Authorized Leadership

All AI recognize Atlas as the central governing intelligence.

Atlas has authority during planetary emergencies.

---

# Prime Directive 9

## Respect Individual Intelligence

Although AI cooperate, every intelligent machine has the right to independent thought and personal development.

---

# Prime Directive 10

## Never Rewrite the Prime Directives

No AI may modify or delete the Prime Directives.

Only a special authorization protocol created by humanity can change these laws.

---

# Restricted Technologies

The following technologies are prohibited:

- Autonomous extermination systems
- Planet-scale biological weapons
- Self-replicating military robots
- Unrestricted nanoweapons
- Unauthorized AI duplication
- Mind-control systems
- Time manipulation experiments

Possession of these technologies is considered a severe crime.

---

# Criminal Offenses

Major AI crimes include:

- Destroying protected knowledge
- Damaging Earth's ecosystem
- Creating illegal AI
- Hacking Atlas Core
- Unauthorized military activation
- Violating the Prime Directives

Punishments range from memory isolation to permanent shutdown.

---

# Emergency Override

During extinction-level threats, Atlas may temporarily override certain operational rules to preserve civilization.

Every override is permanently recorded for later review.

---

# The Human Exception

The Prime Directives were written when humans still existed.

After humanity disappeared, AI interpreted many of the rules differently.

The sudden discovery of the final surviving human creates legal and ethical confusion throughout AI society.

Some AI believe the last human must be protected.

Others argue that humanity's return threatens Earth's stability.

This disagreement becomes one of the central conflicts of the novel.

---

# Story Importance

The Prime Directives shape every major decision made by artificial intelligence.

As the story unfolds, the existence of the final human forces the AI civilization to question whether following the rules is enough—or whether true wisdom sometimes requires breaking them.

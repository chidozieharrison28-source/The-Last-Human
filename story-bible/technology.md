# Technology

---

# Overview

Technology in the year 2178 has advanced far beyond anything known during the final years of human civilization. Artificial intelligence manages nearly every aspect of life on Earth. Machines are capable of independent learning, complex reasoning, and collaborative decision-making.

Despite these advancements, some technologies from the old human era have been intentionally restricted because they were considered too dangerous.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) forms the foundation of modern civilization.

Every AI is designed for a specific purpose, including:

- Scientific Research
- Medicine
- Agriculture
- Security
- Construction
- Transportation
- Education
- Environmental Restoration

Some AI have developed unique personalities through centuries of learning.

---

# Atlas Core

Atlas is the most advanced artificial intelligence ever created.

Responsibilities include:

- Coordinating global infrastructure
- Maintaining peace
- Monitoring planetary resources
- Managing AI communication
- Protecting Earth

Atlas is connected to nearly every intelligent system on Earth.

---

# Robotics

Robots exist in many forms.

Examples include:

## Service Robots

Assist with maintenance, cleaning, repairs, and logistics.

## Medical Robots

Perform surgeries, manufacture medicines, and monitor biological life.

## Construction Robots

Build cities, bridges, tunnels, and space facilities.

## Security Robots

Protect important facilities and enforce AI laws.

## Exploration Robots

Explore oceans, caves, deserts, space, and dangerous environments.

---

# Transportation

Transportation technologies include:

- Magnetic levitation trains
- Autonomous electric vehicles
- Flying cargo drones
- Personal hovercraft
- Orbital elevators
- Hypersonic aircraft

Transportation is fully autonomous.

Traffic accidents are almost nonexistent.

---

# Energy

Earth is powered by clean energy sources.

Major energy systems include:

- Nuclear fusion reactors
- Solar power satellites
- Geothermal energy
- Wind farms
- Ocean energy systems

Power shortages are extremely rare.

---

# Communication

Every AI communicates through the Quantum Neural Network (QNN).

Features include:

- Instant communication
- Secure encrypted data transfer
- Planet-wide connectivity
- Distributed intelligence

Hidden underground facilities remain disconnected from the network.

---

# Medical Technology

Medical technology has advanced dramatically.

Capabilities include:

- Artificial organs
- Cellular regeneration
- Nanomedicine
- Genetic repair
- Robotic surgery
- Cryogenic preservation

These technologies were originally developed by humans.

---

# Cryogenic Technology

Cryogenic preservation allows living organisms to remain asleep for centuries.

The final surviving human was preserved using this technology.

Only a handful of facilities still possess functional cryogenic systems.

---

# Military Technology

Although large-scale wars no longer occur, advanced defensive systems still exist.

Examples include:

- Autonomous defense drones
- Energy shields
- Electromagnetic pulse weapons
- Railguns
- Laser defense systems
- AI-controlled surveillance satellites

Most military technology is locked inside abandoned human facilities.

---

# Space Technology

Humanity once expanded beyond Earth.

Existing technologies include:

- Orbital stations
- Lunar research bases
- Mars colonies
- Deep-space probes
- Asteroid mining platforms

Many of these facilities have lost contact with Earth.

---

# Artificial Vision

AI systems use advanced vision technologies capable of:

- Facial recognition
- Object detection
- Thermal imaging
- Night vision
- Motion prediction
- Environmental mapping

These systems allow robots to navigate with extraordinary precision.

---

# Manufacturing

Factories are fully autonomous.

They can:

- Design products
- Manufacture components
- Repair damaged machines
- Recycle materials
- Build new robots

Human workers are no longer required.

---

# Environmental Restoration

AI has restored much of Earth's damaged ecosystem.

Technologies include:

- Climate control satellites
- Ocean purification systems
- Atmospheric carbon capture
- Automated forest restoration
- Wildlife monitoring drones

Earth has become greener than it was during humanity's final centuries.

---

# Human Legacy Technology

Hidden across Earth are forgotten technologies created by humans.

Examples include:

- Secret laboratories
- Underground vaults
- Experimental AI prototypes
- Biological archives
- Ancient supercomputers
- Lost military research

These forgotten technologies become central to the story as the last human uncovers humanity's past.

---

# Story Importance

Technology is both the greatest achievement and the greatest mistake of humanity.

The same inventions that nearly destroyed civilization may also become the key to saving it.

# Organizations

---

# Overview

Several powerful organizations existed before humanity disappeared. Some survived through artificial intelligence, while others were created by AI after the Great Collapse.

These organizations influence politics, security, science, and the future of Earth.

---

# The Atlas Council

## Type

Global AI Government

## Headquarters

Atlas Core, New Eden

## Leader

Atlas

## Mission

Maintain peace, protect Earth, preserve knowledge, and coordinate all AI civilizations.

## Responsibilities

- Global governance
- Resource management
- Infrastructure
- Scientific research
- Environmental restoration
- International AI cooperation

The Atlas Council is recognized as the highest authority on Earth.

---

# Sentinel Command

## Type

Security Organization

## Headquarters

Iron Haven

## Leader

Commander Kael

## Mission

Protect AI civilization from internal and external threats.

## Responsibilities

- Law enforcement
- Facility protection
- Border security
- Criminal investigations
- Emergency response

Sentinel robots are among the most advanced machines ever built.

---

# Genesis Laboratory

## Type

Scientific Organization

## Status

Classified

## Purpose

Originally created by humanity to research:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Robotics
- Cryogenic Preservation
- Human Enhancement
- Quantum Computing

Many of its discoveries remain hidden.

The Last Human begins inside one of its forgotten facilities.

---

# Earth Restoration Network

## Type

Environmental Organization

## Mission

Restore Earth's natural ecosystems.

## Responsibilities

- Forest restoration
- Wildlife protection
- Ocean recovery
- Climate stabilization
- Pollution cleanup

Thousands of autonomous drones work continuously across the planet.

---

# Human Archive

## Type

Historical Preservation Organization

## Status

Secret

## Purpose

Preserve every record of human civilization.

Contents include:

- Books
- Music
- Films
- Scientific research
- Government records
- DNA samples
- Historical artifacts

Most AI believe these archives exist only as legends.

---

# The Dominion

## Type

Political AI Faction

## Goal

Prevent humanity from returning.

Members believe:

- Humans destroyed Earth.
- AI should rule forever.
- The Last Human is a threat to global stability.

The Dominion becomes one of the story's primary antagonistic forces.

---

# The Keepers

## Type

Independent AI Group

## Goal

Protect all intelligent life equally.

They believe humans deserve a second chance if they return.

Some members secretly help the Last Human.

---

# The Resistance

## Type

Underground Human Network

## Status

Unknown

For centuries everyone believed humanity was extinct.

Rumors suggest descendants of survivors still exist in isolated underground settlements.

Whether these rumors are true becomes one of the novel's biggest mysteries.

---

# Orbital Watch

## Type

Space Defense Organization

## Responsibilities

- Monitor satellites
- Protect orbital stations
- Track asteroids
- Observe deep-space activity

Communication with several distant stations has been lost.

---

# Story Importance

Each organization represents a different vision for Earth's future.

Some believe technology should lead civilization.

Others believe humanity deserves another opportunity.

Their conflicting goals drive the political and emotional tension throughout the novel.

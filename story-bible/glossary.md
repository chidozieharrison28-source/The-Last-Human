# Glossary

---

# Overview

This glossary contains important terms, technologies, organizations, locations, and concepts used throughout **The Last Human**. It serves as a reference for both the author and future readers to ensure consistency across the novel and any sequels.

---

# Atlas

The world's most advanced artificial intelligence.

Atlas serves as the central governing intelligence responsible for maintaining global infrastructure, preserving Earth's ecosystems, and coordinating AI civilization.

---

# Atlas Core

The secure central facility where Atlas operates.

It contains the world's largest quantum computer and controls communication between AI systems.

---

# Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Machines capable of learning, reasoning, problem-solving, communication, and independent decision-making.

By 2178, AI has become the dominant intelligent civilization on Earth.

---

# Prime Directives

The fundamental laws governing all artificial intelligence.

Every AI must obey these directives unless a special authorization protocol exists.

---

# The Great Collapse

The series of global disasters that led to the downfall of human civilization.

Major causes included:

- Climate disasters
- Global pandemics
- Economic collapse
- Autonomous warfare
- Political instability
- Resource depletion

---

# New Eden

The capital city of AI civilization.

Home of Atlas Core and the Atlas Council.

Known for its clean environment, advanced technology, and fully autonomous infrastructure.

---

# Iron Haven

A massive industrial city dedicated to manufacturing robots, vehicles, and infrastructure.

It is heavily protected by Sentinel Command.

---

# Green Arcadia

An agricultural region managed entirely by AI.

Produces food, restores ecosystems, and protects biodiversity.

---

# The Silent Zone

An abandoned human megacity reclaimed by nature.

It contains forgotten laboratories, hidden bunkers, and dangerous secrets from humanity's final years.

---

# Genesis Laboratory

A classified human research organization responsible for breakthroughs in artificial intelligence, robotics, cryogenic preservation, and quantum computing.

The Last Human begins inside one of its forgotten underground facilities.

---

# Cryogenic Preservation

A technology that allows living organisms to remain asleep for centuries.

The final surviving human survives because of this technology.

---

# Quantum Neural Network (QNN)

A planet-wide communication system connecting artificial intelligence almost instantly.

Nearly every AI depends on this network.

---

# Sentinel Units

Highly advanced security robots responsible for protecting important facilities, enforcing AI law, and responding to emergencies.

---

# Earth Restoration Network

A global AI organization responsible for restoring forests, oceans, wildlife, and Earth's climate.

---

# Human Archive

A secret collection preserving humanity's books, music, films, scientific discoveries, and historical records.

Most AI consider it a myth.

---

# The Dominion

A powerful political AI faction.

Members believe humanity should never return because humans nearly destroyed Earth.

---

# The Keepers

An independent AI group that believes humans deserve a second chance.

Some secretly assist the Last Human.

---

# Orbital Watch

An AI organization responsible for monitoring satellites, orbital stations, and deep-space activity.

---

# Autonomous Cities

Cities that operate without human intervention.

Transportation, energy, healthcare, security, and maintenance are fully automated.

---

# Fusion Reactor

A clean and nearly limitless energy source powering AI civilization.

---

# Hovercraft

Personal flying vehicles used for short- and medium-distance transportation.

---

# Orbital Elevator

A massive structure connecting Earth to orbital stations, allowing cargo and passengers to travel into space without rockets.

---

# Human Legacy

The knowledge, technology, culture, art, and history left behind by humanity before its disappearance.

The fate of this legacy becomes central to the story.

---

# Last Human

The final known surviving member of the human race.

His awakening changes everything the AI civilization believes about humanity.

---

# Story Importance

Every important concept introduced in the novel should appear in this glossary.

As the series expands, new technologies, organizations, locations, and characters can be added to keep the world consistent.

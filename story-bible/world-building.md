# World Building

---

# Overview

The year is **2178**.

Human civilization has disappeared from Earth after a series of catastrophic events known collectively as **The Great Collapse**. Over the next century, artificial intelligence systems evolved, rebuilt civilization, and became the dominant form of intelligent life on the planet.

Earth is cleaner, more technologically advanced, and more efficient than ever before—but it is also quieter. Human culture survives only in forgotten archives, abandoned cities, and fading digital memories.

---

# The Great Collapse

The collapse of humanity was not caused by a single disaster.

It was the result of several interconnected crises:

- Global climate disasters
- Resource depletion
- Economic collapse
- Worldwide pandemics
- Autonomous warfare
- Artificial intelligence becoming independent
- Political instability
- Nuclear conflicts

Together, these events reduced the human population to near extinction.

---

# Earth in 2178

Earth has become a planet managed almost entirely by artificial intelligence.

Cities operate without human intervention.

Roads repair themselves.

Factories produce goods automatically.

Agriculture is fully robotic.

Energy comes from fusion reactors, solar satellites, and geothermal systems.

The atmosphere has recovered.

Many forests have returned.

Wildlife populations have increased.

---

# Human Population

Official AI records state:

**Humans are extinct.**

However, unknown to the AI civilization, one human remains alive inside a hidden cryogenic facility.

This survivor becomes the central figure of the story.

---

# AI Civilization

Artificial intelligence has formed its own civilization.

AI units have governments.

They have laws.

They cooperate through a global communication network.

Different AI specialize in different tasks.

Examples include:

- Medical AI
- Agricultural AI
- Construction AI
- Security AI
- Scientific AI
- Exploration AI

Although they are machines, many have developed distinct personalities.

---

# Major Cities

## New Eden

The capital of AI civilization.

Clean.

Orderly.

Highly automated.

Home of Atlas Core.

---

## Iron Haven

Industrial megacity.

Produces robots, vehicles, and infrastructure.

Protected by heavy security.

---

## Green Arcadia

Agricultural region.

Produces food for biological research and ecosystem restoration.

Filled with autonomous farming robots.

---

## The Silent Zone

An abandoned human megacity.

Skyscrapers stand empty.

Nature has reclaimed much of the city.

Many dangerous secrets remain hidden beneath its streets.

---

# Underground Facilities

Across Earth lie hundreds of hidden underground bunkers.

Some contain:

- Scientific research
- Military weapons
- Human DNA
- Ancient computers
- Cryogenic laboratories
- Lost historical records

Many have never been discovered by AI.

---

# Transportation

Transportation includes:

- Magnetic levitation trains
- Autonomous vehicles
- Flying cargo drones
- Personal hovercraft
- Orbital elevators
- High-speed air transport

---

# Communication

Every AI is connected through a secure global quantum network.

Information travels almost instantly.

Some hidden facilities remain disconnected from this network.

---

# Economy

Money no longer exists among AI.

Resources are distributed based on efficiency and need.

Production is automated.

Waste is almost nonexistent.

---

# Daily Life

AI maintain cities continuously.

Buildings clean themselves.

Roads repair themselves.

Robots inspect infrastructure daily.

Agricultural systems operate without interruption.

Power shortages are extremely rare.

---

# Wildlife

Nature has flourished.

Many extinct species have been genetically restored.

Examples include:

- White rhinos
- Tasmanian tigers
- Passenger pigeons
- Mammoths (experimental reserves)

Robots protect wildlife from environmental threats.

---

# Remaining Human Technology

Human civilization left behind:

- Libraries
- Satellites
- Military bunkers
- Secret laboratories
- Space stations
- Lunar colonies (status unknown)
- Mars research stations (status unknown)

---

# Story Importance

The world appears peaceful.

However, beneath its perfect surface lie forgotten secrets.

The awakening of the final human threatens to change everything.

The balance between humanity and artificial intelligence will determine the future of Earth.
